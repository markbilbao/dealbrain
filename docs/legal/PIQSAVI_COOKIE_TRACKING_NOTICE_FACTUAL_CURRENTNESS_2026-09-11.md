# PiqSavi Cookie & Tracking Notice

## 1. Purpose

This notice explains PiqSavi's current use, and possible future use, of cookies, browser storage, analytics, affiliate tracking, advertising technologies, and similar technologies in the consumer service.

This Notice applies to tracking technologies controlled or implemented by PiqSavi. Tracking technologies operated independently by third parties are governed by the applicable third party's own privacy and tracking practices.

## 2. Current Storage and Session Posture

PiqSavi does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies, tracking pixels, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, advertising pixels, or non-essential tracking. Authentication is currently designed around token-based sessions rather than browser cookie-based sessions.

The consumer application currently uses first-party cookies and browser storage that are necessary or functional for the product:

- HTTP cookies named `piqsavi_decision_owner`, `piqsavi_delivery`, and `piqsavi_shopping_market`
- `sessionStorage` or `localStorage` for an access token and remember-me preference
- `sessionStorage` for a shopping-assistant conversation identifier

Those first-party stores are not session-authentication cookies and are not advertising or analytics cookies. The Early Access landing page does not currently use those cookies or browser-storage keys.

PiqSavi does not currently operate a cookie-consent banner or consent-management platform because advertising and analytics technologies are not currently active.

## 3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that websites may store in a browser. Local storage and session storage are browser-storage mechanisms that can remember information on a device. Pixels or tags can record that a page, message, or action was viewed. These technologies may be used for security, preferences, analytics, advertising, or referral attribution where implemented.

## 4. Strictly Necessary and Security Technologies

PiqSavi may use technical mechanisms necessary to operate, protect, authenticate, or secure the service. The first-party cookies and browser-storage keys listed in section 2 are the current necessary or functional product stores. If additional browser cookies or similar technologies are introduced for these purposes, this notice should be updated to identify them accurately.

## 5. Preferences and Functional Storage

Account preferences are currently designed to be stored primarily on the server when tied to an authenticated account. The consumer application also uses the first-party browser-storage keys listed in section 2 for access-token continuity, remember-me preference, and shopping-assistant conversation continuity. Those stores are not the primary account-preference database.

## 6. Analytics

PiqSavi does not currently use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, or a comparable consumer analytics cookie or pixel stack in the consumer experience reflected by these documents. If analytics technologies are introduced, this notice should be updated with the relevant provider, purposes, and user choices.

## 7. Advertising and Targeting

PiqSavi does not currently use advertising pixels or a third-party display or programmatic advertising stack in the consumer experience reflected by these documents. If targeted advertising or similar technologies are introduced, PiqSavi should update this notice and any required preference or consent controls.

## 8. Affiliate Tracking

Where an approved affiliate program is active, referral links may include identifiers that allow the merchant or affiliate provider to recognize a referral. The merchant or affiliate provider may use its own cookies or tracking technologies after you leave PiqSavi. Those technologies are controlled by the third party and are subject to its own privacy and cookie practices.

PiqSavi does not currently activate affiliate tracking on the Early Access landing page or in the consumer application.

PiqSavi does not control the tracking technologies operated by the merchant or affiliate network after you leave PiqSavi.

The applicable provider may use cookies, device identifiers, server-side identifiers, pixels, or other technologies to recognize referrals or measure transactions, subject to its own privacy notice, program terms, and applicable law.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Third-party infrastructure, email, merchant, affiliate, AI, or other service providers may use technologies within their own services. PiqSavi does not control technologies placed by a third party on the third party's own website or application after you navigate away from PiqSavi.

## 10. Logs Are Not the Same as Cookies

PiqSavi may create server-side request, security, or operational logs. Server-side logging is different from placing a cookie or browser-storage item on your device.

## 11. Browser Controls

Most browsers allow users to delete or block cookies and other browser storage. Blocking necessary technologies could affect features that depend on them. Third-party merchant websites may have their own browser-storage controls and notices.

## 12. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Signals

PiqSavi does not currently describe a dedicated consumer mechanism that changes service behavior in response to browser Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals. If PiqSavi later implements such a mechanism, this notice should be updated.

## 13. Retention

Because advertising and analytics cookies are not currently active, PiqSavi does not currently publish a live inventory of those cookie retention periods. The current first-party HTTP cookies listed in section 2 are session cookies. Any future cookie, analytics, advertising, or affiliate tracking inventory should state the relevant retention or expiration period where known.

## 14. Changes to This Notice

PiqSavi may update this notice when browser storage, analytics, affiliate tracking, advertising, consent controls, or related technologies change. The Last Updated date will be revised when this notice changes.
