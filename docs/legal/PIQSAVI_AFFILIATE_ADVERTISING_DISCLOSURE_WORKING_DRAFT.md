# PiqSavi Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure

**Last Updated: August 25, 2026**


## 1. Purpose

This disclosure explains how PiqSavi may earn revenue from affiliate relationships and how PiqSavi will distinguish affiliate, advertising, or sponsored commercial relationships from organic shopping recommendations.

PiqSavi may participate in affiliate, referral, advertising, sponsored, or other commercial programs involving merchants, marketplaces, affiliate networks, or other third parties.

This Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure explains how those relationships may affect links, recommendations, advertising, and compensation on PiqSavi.

This Disclosure supplements the PiqSavi Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. It does not replace the terms or policies of any third-party merchant, marketplace, affiliate network, or other service to which you may be directed.


## 2. Affiliate Relationships

PiqSavi may use affiliate links or referral-attribution mechanisms. When an applicable affiliate relationship is active and a qualifying purchase or other qualifying event occurs, PiqSavi may receive compensation from the merchant, marketplace, affiliate network, or other program. Not every merchant link or destination is necessarily an affiliate link.

An application to an affiliate program, technical integration, credentials, tracking identifiers, or access to product data does not by itself mean that PiqSavi has permission for every commercial or data use. Permissions may vary by merchant, program, market, capability, and source.


## 3. Ranking Neutrality

Affiliate compensation must not increase PiqScore or organic Recommendation ranking. PiqScore evaluates the offer. Recommendation reflects the buying decision logic and, where applicable, shopper fit. Affiliate attachment is designed to occur after selection and ranking rather than influencing which offer wins.


## 4. How Affiliate Links May Work

When an affiliate link is active, the outbound link or referral may contain identifiers that allow a merchant or affiliate program to recognize the referral. Depending on the approved provider and configuration, those identifiers may support attribution of a qualifying purchase or other qualifying event.

The merchant or affiliate provider controls its own attribution rules, qualifying-event rules, commission terms, cookie or tracking practices, and payout rules.

Whether a referral results in compensation, and the amount of any compensation, is determined by the applicable merchant, affiliate network, or program rules. PiqSavi does not control the merchant's attribution process and cannot guarantee that a click, referral, order, purchase, registration, or other action will be attributed to PiqSavi.


## 5. User Price

PiqSavi does not add an affiliate fee to the price shown by the merchant. A merchant or affiliate program may independently set prices, discounts, promotions, or other terms. You should verify the final price and conditions on the merchant's own service before purchasing.


## 6. Advertising and Sponsored Placements

If PiqSavi introduces paid advertising or sponsored placements, paid commercial content should be identified so that users can distinguish it from organic PiqScore and Recommendation results. A paid placement must not be presented as though payment caused it to have a higher objective PiqScore or organic Recommendation ranking.


## 7. AI and Commercial Relationships

AI-assisted explanations must not rewrite canonical objective PiqScore because of affiliate compensation. If sponsored or paid commercial content is introduced into conversational or AI-assisted surfaces, it should be clearly distinguishable from organic shopping guidance.


## 8. Tracking and Privacy

Affiliate or advertising technologies may process referral, click, source, campaign, merchant, product, device, country, or similar attribution information when those technologies are active. PiqSavi's Privacy Policy and Cookie & Tracking Notice describe privacy and tracking practices more broadly.

Affiliate links may contain tracking information supplied or required by the applicable merchant or affiliate program. After you leave PiqSavi, the applicable merchant, affiliate network, or other third party may use cookies or similar technologies to recognize referrals or measure transactions, subject to that party's own privacy and tracking practices.

PiqSavi does not represent that every affiliate link uses cookies, that every click results in attribution, or that a particular attribution period applies universally.


## 9. Third-Party Merchants and Programs

Merchants, marketplaces, affiliate networks, and advertising providers may have their own terms, disclosure requirements, privacy practices, eligibility rules, and attribution technologies. References to a merchant or program do not by themselves imply approval, partnership, or permission for every capability.

Participation in an affiliate or referral program does not mean that PiqSavi controls or guarantees the merchant's products, prices, inventory, fulfillment, returns, refunds, warranties, customer service, or transaction processing.

PiqSavi may also be required by an applicable merchant or affiliate program to remove, modify, restrict, or discontinue particular links, products, content, promotional materials, or functionality.


## 10. Market and Source Coverage

PiqSavi does not claim universal merchant, affiliate-program, or market coverage. A relationship or permission in one market, source, or capability does not automatically apply to another.


## 11. Outbound Purchases

Purchases reached through PiqSavi are generally completed with the third-party merchant. The merchant controls checkout, payment, final price, inventory, fulfillment, returns, refunds, warranty, and related transaction terms.


## 12. Identifying Affiliate or Sponsored Content

Where an affiliate relationship is relevant to a user action, PiqSavi may use clear labels such as "Affiliate link," "PiqSavi may earn a commission," or similar plain-language wording. Sponsored or paid placements, if introduced, should also be clearly labeled near the relevant content or action.


## 13. Social, Creator, and Promotional Content

If PiqSavi or PiqSavi-branded media later uses affiliate links, sponsorships, gifted products, creator campaigns, or similar promotional relationships, those relationships should be disclosed in the relevant content when applicable.


## 14. Changes to This Disclosure

PiqSavi may update this disclosure as monetization methods, providers, programs, or the service change. The Last Updated date will be revised when this disclosure changes.
