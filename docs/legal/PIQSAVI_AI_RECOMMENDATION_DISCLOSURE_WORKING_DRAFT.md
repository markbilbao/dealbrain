# PiqSavi AI & Recommendation Disclosure

**Last Updated: August 25, 2026**


## 1. Purpose

This disclosure explains what PiqScore means, how Recommendations differ from PiqScore, how personalization may affect recommendations, how AI may assist with explanations, and the limitations users should understand before relying on scores, recommendations, or AI-assisted content.


## 2. PiqScore

PiqScore is PiqSavi's objective offer-evaluation score. It evaluates a specific buying opportunity using information available to PiqSavi for that evaluation. PiqScore is scoped to the offers PiqSavi actually evaluated and does not imply complete coverage of the market.

Missing, unknown, incomplete, imported, test, simulated, cached, or stale information may affect evaluation quality.

PiqScore is designed to follow deterministic scoring rules.

AI is not designed to rewrite canonical objective PiqScore.

Affiliate compensation must not increase PiqScore.


## 3. Recommendations

Recommendation is a separate customer-action layer. PiqSavi may provide guidance such as Buy, Wait, Consider, or Avoid, depending on the information and rules available for the decision. Recommendation is not the same thing as PiqScore.

A recommendation may consider thresholds, warnings, tradeoffs, confidence, incomplete information, and other decision rules. A Recommendation may therefore differ from simply choosing the highest PiqScore offer.

A Recommendation does not constitute a guarantee that a product is the best product for you, the lowest-priced offer, the safest product, the highest-quality product, or the most suitable product for your particular circumstances.


## 4. Personalization

Where account preferences or related signals are available, personalization may influence which option is personally recommended. Personalization is designed not to rewrite the canonical objective PiqScore. PiqScore evaluates the offer; the personally recommended choice reflects the shopper's fit where supported.


## 5. AI-Assisted Explanations

PiqSavi may use AI to help explain results, summarize evidence, compare tradeoffs, answer follow-up questions, or provide conversational shopping assistance. AI-generated content may contain errors, omissions, incomplete reasoning, or outdated information. AI output may not be human-reviewed.

Where AI is used, it is intended to support explanation and reasoning rather than secretly changing the canonical objective scoring rules.


## 6. Evidence and Market Coverage

PiqSavi's conclusions are limited by the information and sources actually evaluated. PiqSavi does not claim to search every marketplace, every merchant, every review source, or the entire internet. A "Best Piq" or similar recommendation should be understood as the best result among the offers PiqSavi actually evaluated for that decision, not a guarantee of universal market completeness.


## 7. Unknown or Incomplete Information

PiqSavi may not always know shipping, taxes, duties, import costs, warranty coverage, returns, seller trust, inventory, or freshness. Unknown information should remain identified as unknown rather than being silently treated as zero or complete. Missing information may change the usefulness or confidence of a recommendation.


## 8. Affiliate Neutrality

Affiliate compensation must not increase canonical PiqScore or organic Recommendation ranking. An affiliate-linked destination and an organic recommendation are related product surfaces with different rules.

Where PiqSavi receives compensation from a merchant, marketplace, affiliate network, or other commercial partner, that commercial relationship is separate from the methodology used for organic PiqScore and Recommendations.

Where a placement is sponsored, paid, or otherwise commercially promoted, PiqSavi will identify or present that placement separately from organic Recommendations as required by applicable law and the applicable commercial arrangement.


## 9. No Guarantee of Outcome

PiqScore, Recommendations, personalization, and AI-assisted explanations are decision-support tools. They do not guarantee the lowest price, savings, product suitability, merchant performance, availability, delivery, warranty outcomes, or any other purchase result. Users should review material merchant and product details before purchasing.


## 10. Higher-Risk Decisions

PiqSavi is designed for shopping assistance, not professional financial, medical, legal, investment, or other regulated advice. Users should obtain appropriate professional guidance when a decision involves regulated, safety-critical, or high-risk matters.


## 11. Changes to This Disclosure

PiqSavi may update this disclosure as scoring, recommendation, personalization, AI, or data-source behavior changes. The Last Updated date will be revised when this disclosure changes.
