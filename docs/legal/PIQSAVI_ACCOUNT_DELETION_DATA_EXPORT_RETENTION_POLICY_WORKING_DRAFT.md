# PiqSavi Account Deletion, Data Export & Retention Policy

**Last Updated: August 25, 2026**


## 1. Purpose

This Policy explains how users may request deletion of account-related information, request access to or an export of their data, what categories of account-related information may exist, and the current retention posture of the PiqSavi consumer service.


## 2. Account Data and Activity

Account identity information such as user identifier, email address, display name, account timestamps, and account status information.

Authentication and security information such as password hashes, session-token hashes, session expiry information, and security events.

Preferences and settings such as budget, currency, country preference, theme, language, AI-mode preferences, and notification preferences.

Saved products and related activity, including comparisons, searches, recommendation history, recently viewed items, favorites, wishlist items, owned products, or accessories where those features are used.

Recommendation or personalization-related account state where used.

Affiliate click or attribution information where an approved affiliate path is active.

Support and privacy correspondence.

Security, audit, and operational logs.


## 3. Current Deletion Capability

PiqSavi may allow deletion of individual saved items. A complete self-service account-deletion workflow, automated account-wide purge, or instant erasure from every log, backup, or third-party system may not be available.

Removing an individual saved item does not necessarily delete all other account, audit, attribution, security, backup, or third-party information associated with the account.


## 4. Current Data Export Capability

A complete automated self-service personal-data archive or portability download may not be available. Authenticated account screens or APIs may expose portions of account information, but that is not the same as a complete data-export package.


## 5. How to Request Deletion, Access, or Export

Send privacy-related deletion, access, correction, or export requests to privacy@piqsavi.com. General product support is available at support@piqsavi.com. Privacy requests may be handled manually.


## 6. Identity Verification

PiqSavi may need to verify that a requester is authorized to act on an account before fulfilling a deletion, access, correction, or export request. Verification may include confirming control of the account email or using other account-authentication information. Additional verification may be required where necessary to protect users against fraud or unauthorized requests.


## 7. Retention

Certain records use technical expiration periods for security or operational purposes. For example, sessions and authentication-related tokens may expire or be revoked after defined periods. These technical expiries are not the same thing as a complete privacy retention schedule.

A final public category-by-category retention schedule is not yet established for all account, saved-activity, audit, affiliate, support, and backup records. PiqSavi may need to retain limited information when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal process, dispute handling, or other obligations, subject to applicable law and the final retention framework adopted for the service.


## 8. Sessions and Security Records

Session records may include session identifiers, hashed tokens, expiry times, remember-me behavior, last-seen time, and revocation state. Logout or expiry may invalidate a session. Security or audit records may be retained separately from active session state.


## 9. Backups and Third-Party Systems

Deletion from active systems may not always result in immediate removal from every backup, security log, email system, merchant or affiliate provider, AI provider, or other third-party system. Treatment of those systems depends on the service involved and the final deletion and retention procedures adopted for PiqSavi.


## 10. Data Export Content

Where PiqSavi provides an access or export response, the content may include account and profile fields, preferences and settings, saved items and related account activity, recommendation-related account information, and privacy-request information where applicable. Security-sensitive or proprietary information such as password hashes, session-token hashes, internal security signals, or proprietary scoring internals may be excluded where appropriate.


## 11. Request Timing

PiqSavi will handle privacy requests through the available privacy contact and subject to applicable law, identity verification, operational constraints, and any permitted exceptions. Response timing may vary depending on the request and the applicable legal requirements.


## 12. Children and Representatives

PiqSavi does not currently use date of birth as a standard registration field or operate a coded age gate in the consumer service reflected by these documents. Requests submitted by a parent, guardian, authorized agent, or other representative may require additional verification of authority.


## 13. Changes to This Policy

PiqSavi may update this Policy as deletion, export, retention, backup, and privacy-request processes change. The Last Updated date will be revised when this Policy changes.


## 14. Contact

Privacy: privacy@piqsavi.com

General support: support@piqsavi.com
