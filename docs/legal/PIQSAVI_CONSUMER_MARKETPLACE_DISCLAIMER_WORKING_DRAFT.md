**Last Updated: August 25, 2026**


## 1. Purpose

This disclaimer explains important limitations concerning product and offer information, merchant and source information, prices, availability, ratings and reviews, seller information, shipping, returns, warranties, PiqScore, Recommendations, personalization, AI explanations, outbound merchant links, and third-party purchases.

PiqSavi is a shopping and recommendation service designed to help users compare and evaluate products and offers. PiqSavi is generally designed as a shopping-intelligence and referral-oriented service rather than the seller, manufacturer, distributor, payment processor, shipping carrier, or warranty provider for third-party goods.

Unless expressly stated otherwise for a particular service, PiqSavi is not the seller, manufacturer, distributor, payment processor, fulfillment provider, shipping carrier, warranty provider, or operator of the third-party marketplace through which a product or service may be purchased.

PiqSavi does not have authority to make representations, warranties, commitments, or other obligations on behalf of a third-party merchant unless expressly authorized in writing by that merchant


## 3. Third-Party Transactions

Purchases reached through PiqSavi may be completed with third-party merchants. The merchant may control checkout, payment, final price, inventory, shipping, returns, refunds, warranty, and customer service. The merchant's own terms and policies may apply.

Naming or displaying a merchant, marketplace, seller, or provider does not by itself imply a contractual relationship, approval, endorsement, or live certified integration.

The terms presented by the applicable merchant at the time of purchase govern the transaction. Information displayed by PiqSavi is provided to assist comparison and evaluation and does not replace the merchant's own transaction terms.

PiqSavi does not guarantee that a merchant will accept an order, maintain an advertised price, honor a promotion, maintain inventory, complete a transaction, provide a refund, or otherwise perform as expected.


## 4. Product and Offer Information

Product and offer information may come from third parties, imports, cached data, test data, simulated sources, public sources, or other available evidence. Information may be incomplete, unavailable, delayed, stale, or incorrect. PiqSavi does not guarantee complete catalog coverage or perfect accuracy.

Product and offer information may also be subject to source-specific presentation, refresh, attribution, licensing, access, or other restrictions imposed by the relevant merchant, marketplace, affiliate program, API provider, or other data source.

PiqSavi may therefore limit, modify, refresh, remove, or discontinue particular information, fields, links, or features where required by the applicable source or authorization.


## 5. Price

Prices can change between the time PiqSavi evaluates an offer and the time you visit or purchase from a merchant. Discounts, vouchers, taxes, shipping, duties, import costs, payment fees, or other unavoidable charges may affect what you actually pay. When material costs are unknown, PiqSavi may not be able to provide a complete final effective cost.

Price and availability information may be based on information retrieved or refreshed at a particular time and may change before or during checkout. Where a merchant or applicable program requires a date/time indication, freshness statement, or other price or availability qualification, PiqSavi may display such information or qualification as required by the applicable program.

The merchant's price and availability information applicable at the time of purchase will govern the transaction.


## 6. Availability and Inventory

Availability and inventory may change without notice. An offer shown by PiqSavi may become unavailable, change seller, change variant, or change purchase conditions before checkout.

A merchant may withdraw, restrict, suspend, or change the availability of a product or offer at any time.


## 7. Shipping and Delivery

Shipping cost, delivery time, destination eligibility, taxes, duties, import charges, and other delivery-related information may vary by location and may not always be known to PiqSavi. Verify the final delivery terms with the merchant before purchasing.


## 8. Returns, Refunds, and Warranties

Returns, refunds, warranty, repair, exchange, and statutory product rights may depend on the merchant, manufacturer, product, location, and applicable law. PiqSavi does not promise to provide those post-purchase services for third-party purchases.

Where you purchase from a third-party merchant, the merchant's applicable cancellation, return, refund, exchange, and dispute policies generally apply to that purchase, subject to any mandatory rights or remedies provided by applicable law.

PiqSavi does not determine whether a particular order qualifies for cancellation, return, refund, exchange, or other remedy unless PiqSavi expressly provides such a service.

Any warranty, guarantee, statutory remedy, or product assurance applicable to a purchase is determined by the applicable merchant, seller, manufacturer, applicable program, and applicable law.


## 9. Sellers and Merchant Trust

Seller or merchant trust indicators may be incomplete or unavailable. A seller rating, official-store indicator, marketplace badge, review count, or similar signal does not guarantee authenticity, fulfillment quality, customer service, or future conduct.


## 10. Ratings, Reviews, and Third-Party Opinions

Ratings, reviews, community posts, videos, comments, or other third-party opinions may be subjective, incomplete, manipulated, outdated, sponsored, or unrelated to your specific needs. PiqSavi may summarize or use such information as evidence but does not guarantee its accuracy or independence.


## 11. PiqScore

PiqScore is an objective evaluation of a specific offer using the information PiqSavi actually evaluated. It does not guarantee that the offer is the best deal available anywhere, the lowest price in the market, or suitable for every user.


## 12. Recommendations and Personalization

Recommendation is separate from PiqScore. A personally recommended option may differ from the offer with the highest objective PiqScore because preferences, thresholds, tradeoffs, warnings, confidence, or other recommendation rules may affect shopper fit. Recommendations are decision support, not a guarantee of outcome.


## 13. AI Explanations

AI-assisted explanations may contain errors, omissions, incomplete reasoning, or outdated information and may not be human-reviewed. AI is not designed to rewrite canonical objective PiqScore. Users should verify material product and merchant facts before purchasing.


## 14. Source and Market Coverage

PiqSavi does not claim to search every marketplace, merchant, product page, review source, community, or other source. A recommendation such as "Best Piq" should be understood as the best result among the offers PiqSavi actually evaluated for that decision, not as proof of universal market completeness.


## 15. Unknown or Unavailable Data

Some fields may be unknown or unavailable, including shipping, taxes, duties, import charges, seller trust, warranty, returns, freshness, or stock. Unknown information should not be assumed to be zero, free, favorable, or complete. Missing information may materially change the value of an offer or recommendation.
