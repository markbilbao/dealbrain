PiqSavi is a shopping and recommendation service designed to help users compare and evaluate products and offers. Among other features, PiqSavi may:

- evaluate offers using **PiqScore**;
- present separate **Recommendations**;
- apply **personalization** where account preferences or related signals are available;
- provide **AI-assisted explanations** where that path is enabled; and

### What PiqSavi is **not** described as (based on current product model)

- the seller of the underlying goods;
- the manufacturer;
- the distributor;
- the payment processor for third-party retail checkout;
- the shipping carrier; or
- the warranty provider for merchant products.

Based on the intended product model reviewed for this draft, PiqSavi is generally designed as a shopping-intelligence / referral-oriented service rather than the retail seller of the underlying goods.

## 12. PiqScore

**PiqScore** is PiqSavi’s public name for an **objective evaluation of offers** based on available information and the offers PiqSavi actually evaluated.

Depending on available evidence, inputs may include cost-related signals, seller rating, shipping, availability, official-store indicators, warranty-related attributes, returns-related attributes, and similar offer factors where present.

### Important PiqScore limitations

PiqScore does **not** mean:

- the globally best offer;
- the lowest price everywhere;
- a guaranteed best product;
- guaranteed merchant reliability;
- guaranteed future savings; or
- complete market coverage.

Unknown, missing, incomplete, demonstration, imported, simulated, or stale information may affect PiqScore quality. Missing information is **not** the same as a verified negative finding about a merchant or product.

### Architecture boundaries (preserve)

- **PiqScore** is distinct from **Recommendation**.
- **Personalization** may influence a personally recommended choice or personalized PiqScore-style result **without rewriting** canonical objective PiqScore.
- **AI** is designed for explanation/reasoning assistance and does **not** rewrite canonical PiqScore.
- **Affiliate compensation must not increase** PiqScore or organic Recommendation ranking.

This section does **not** disclose proprietary scoring formulas, weights, or implementation details.

Cross-reference: **PiqSavi AI & Recommendation Disclosure** (counsel draft; not treated here as a published final disclosure).

Recommendations are intended to **assist**—not replace—your own purchasing judgment. This draft does **not** guarantee suitability, savings, merchant performance, or purchase outcomes.
